ABOUT US

The Baghgadi Story

Founded with a passion for excellence, Baghgadi Fabrics (FB Fabrics) has been serving customers with the finest quality unstitched fabrics for over 15 years. Our journey began with a simple vision — to bring luxury and tradition together in every thread we offer.

From premium lawn collections to luxurious chiffon, warm khaddar to elegant silk, we curate only the best fabrics that meet international quality standards. Each piece in our collection is carefully selected to ensure you receive nothing but perfection.

Our commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and timely delivery has made us a trusted name in the fabric industry. We believe every woman deserves to feel elegant, and that's what we deliver — elegance in every thread.
